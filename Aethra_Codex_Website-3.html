Part I of XIV

Cosmological Structure

The Four Realms

1. The Divine Heavens (Upper Realm)

Location: Exists above the mortal plane, accessible only through celestial rifts or divine intervention.

Nature: A realm of pure light, celestial harmony, and ordered creation. Home to Celestials, Nephilim, and the avatars of divine concepts.

Key Features

  • The Eternal Forge where stars are birthed

  • The Celestial Archive recording all of existence

  • The Hall of Ascendance where mortals may transcend

2. The Mortal Plane (Surface & Skies)

Location: The primary setting, encompassing eight continents across diverse climates.

Nature: A realm of balance between order and chaos, light and shadow.

Key Features

  • Eight distinct continents

  • Floating sky-islands known as The Shattered Reach

  • Vast oceans teeming with life

  • Deep forests and burning deserts

3. The Deep Underworld (10,000-15,000 km Below)

Location: Accessible through the Maw, deep-sea trenches, and glacial rifts.

Nature: A realm of primal darkness, ancient beings, and forgotten civilizations.

Key Features

  • The Obsidian Vaults

  • The Caverns of Eternal Night

  • The Heartstone at the core of the world

  • Gates leading to the Dark Lands

4. The Dark Lands (Shadow Realm)

Location: A parallel dimension overlapping the mortal plane, accessible through shadow.

Nature: A realm of pure corruption, distorted reality, and eldritch horrors.

Key Features

  • The Void Gates

  • The Screaming Plains

  • The Throne of Night

Part III of XIV

The Grand System of Arcadia (Magic)

I. Elemental & Physical Arts

1. Pyromancy (Fire Magic)

Pyromancy encompasses three distinct subschools of fire magic.

Combustion

This subschool focuses on explosive, high-heat bursts, pressure waves, and rapid ignition.

Practitioners learn to create flames that detonate with concussive force, generating shockwaves that can shatter stone and send enemies flying. The magic manifests as sudden conflagrations and controlled explosions, allowing the caster to unleash devastating area attacks or precise bursts of flame.

Key abilities include generating bolts of fire, creating explosive waves that push enemies back, crafting delayed explosive runes that trigger when disturbed, and summoning massive fireballs that engulf entire areas in searing flames. More advanced practitioners can create chain reactions that leap between multiple targets or unleash volcanic blasts that create zones of molten devastation.

Thermal Control

This subschool focuses on regulating temperature, siphoning ambient heat, and insulating against cold or flame. Practitioners gain precise control over thermal energy, able to draw heat from their surroundings to create zones of bitter cold or channel warmth to protect allies from freezing environments.

Key abilities include warming surfaces to comfort levels, creating zones of extreme cold, generating protective barriers against temperature extremes, and sensing heat signatures to track hidden enemies. Advanced practitioners can absorb heat from enemies to heal themselves or create bubbles of comfortable temperature in hostile environments. The most skilled can achieve temperatures approaching absolute zero.

Infernal Weaving

This subschool shapes fire into persistent structures, fiery constructs, and sentient flame beasts.

Practitioners learn to give form to fire, creating weapons, guardians, and servants from pure flame.

Key abilities include shaping flames into whips and walls, constructing temporary fire servants, creating cages of flame to imprison foes, and summoning burning beasts to fight alongside the caster. Advanced practitioners can create powerful fire elementals, armor themselves in flame, and even summon dragon-like constructs made entirely of living fire.

2. Hydromancy (Water Magic)

Hydromancy encompasses three distinct subschools of water magic.

Aquatic Control

This subschool manipulates physical bodies of water, tides, currents, and high-pressure liquid jets. Practitioners gain command over water in all its forms, able to shape it, move it, and weaponize it.

Key abilities include creating and shaping water, generating high-pressure jets that can cut through stone, creating tidal waves that sweep enemies away, and controlling larger bodies of water such as rivers and lakes. Advanced practitioners can transform their bodies into liquid form, create devastating maelstroms, and summon massive floods that reshape entire landscapes.

Cryomancy

This subschool freezes liquids, forms ice weapons, creates sub-zero barriers, and controls frost.

Practitioners learn to draw heat from water, transforming it into ice and using it for offense and defense.

Key abilities include creating ice weapons and armor, generating walls of ice for protection, crafting ice prisons to trap enemies, and summoning blizzards that blind and harm foes.

Advanced practitioners can create powerful ice constructs, generate storms of freezing destruction, and create permanent ice structures.

Vapor Shaping

This subschool controls fog, steam, humidity, and atmospheric condensation for concealment or scalding attacks. Practitioners gain mastery over water in its gaseous form.

Key abilities include creating concealing fogs, generating scalding steam that burns enemies, transforming into mist to avoid damage, and creating acid fogs that corrode and poison.

Advanced practitioners can create vast fields of concealment and unleash devastating steam explosions.

3. Geomancy (Earth Magic)

Geomancy encompasses three distinct subschools of earth magic.

Terramancy

This subschool shapes soil, stone, and rock formations for defensive walls, earth spikes, or terraforming. Practitioners gain command over the earth itself.

Key abilities include shaping earth and stone, creating defensive walls and barriers, generating spikes that impale enemies, and causing tremors that disrupt enemy movements. Advanced practitioners can create powerful stone guardians, unleash devastating earthquakes, and reshape terrain on a massive scale.

Ferromancy

This subschool manipulates refined and raw metals, magnetic properties, and metallic alloys.

Practitioners gain control over the metals that lie within the earth.

Key abilities include attracting and repelling metal objects, corroding metal armor and weapons, shaping metal into forms, and creating storms of metal shards. Advanced practitioners can control metal objects telekinetically, create powerful iron constructs, and unleash magnetic pulses that devastate metal-clad enemies.

Crystallomancy

This subschool grows and weaponizes dense crystalline structures, prism reflections, and resonance gems. Practitioners learn to harness the power of crystals.

Key abilities include creating crystal shards, generating crystal armor, crafting crystal prisons, and using prismatic reflections to unleash beams of light. Advanced practitioners can create crystal forests, unleash devastating prismatic sprays, and use resonance to shatter structures.

4. Aeromancy (Air & Wind Magic)

Aeromancy encompasses three distinct subschools of air magic.

Gale Manipulation

This subschool controls wind pressure, creates vacuums, directs flight currents, and summons gusts. Practitioners gain mastery over the winds.

Key abilities include creating gusts that push enemies, generating protective air shields, creating vacuums that suffocate, and using wind to propel flight. Advanced practitioners can create devastating tornadoes, unleash vacuum waves that tear enemies apart, and summon storms of vengeance.

Acoustic Weaving

This subschool manipulates sound waves, sonic booms, silence bubbles, and voice mimicking.

Practitioners learn to harness sound itself.

Key abilities include creating sonic pulses that harm and disorient, generating bubbles of silence, mimicking voices, and using sound to locate hidden enemies. Advanced practitioners can unleash devastating sonic screams, create walls of sound, and use harmonic resonance to shatter structures.

Atmospheric Control

This subschool alters local air composition, humidity, barometric pressure, and localized microclimates. Practitioners gain command over the atmosphere itself.

Key abilities include purifying air, altering oxygen levels, modifying weather, and creating toxic clouds. Advanced practitioners can create zones of suffocation, control weather over vast areas, and unleash atmospheric devastation that ravages entire regions.

5. Fulgurmancy (Lightning & Electricity)

Fulgurmancy encompasses three distinct subschools of lightning magic.

Volt Manipulation

This subschool generates raw electric arcs, lightning bolts, and sudden static discharge.

Practitioners gain command over lightning itself.

Key abilities include creating bolts of lightning that leap between targets, generating protective fields of static, and unleashing devastating lightning storms. Advanced practitioners can summon lightning elementals and unleash apocalyptic thunderbolts.

Electromagnetism

This subschool controls magnetic fields, metallic polarity, and bio-electric signals. Practitioners learn to harness the electromagnetic forces.

Key abilities include creating magnetic fields that attract or repel metal, sensing life through bio-electric signatures, and using magnetic forces to immobilize armored enemies. Advanced practitioners can create devastating electromagnetic storms.

Plasma Crafting

This subschool focuses electricity into superheated plasma blades, ball lightning, and ionized barriers. Practitioners learn to transform lightning into its hottest form.

Key abilities include creating plasma spheres and blades, summoning ball lightning, and generating ionized barriers. Advanced practitioners can create powerful plasma constructs and unleash annihilation-level attacks.

II. Life & Organic Arts

6. Biomancy (Body & Flesh Magic)

Biomancy encompasses three distinct subschools of body magic.

Morphology

This subschool alters physical biology, shaping bone, muscle density, and claws or wings.

Practitioners learn to reshape living flesh.

Key abilities include creating bone spurs and armor, enhancing muscle density and strength, growing claws and wings, and transforming into other forms. Advanced practitioners can completely alter their biology and achieve apotheosis.

Regeneration

This subschool accelerates cellular growth to rapidly heal wounds, cure diseases, and restore tissue. Practitioners gain mastery over healing.

Key abilities include healing wounds, curing poison and disease, regenerating lost limbs, and restoring the dead to life. Advanced practitioners can achieve true regeneration and resurrection.

Somatic Alteration

This subschool enhances physical attributes beyond natural limits. Practitioners learn to push the body beyond its normal capabilities.

Key abilities include enhancing senses, increasing speed, boosting strength, and achieving darkvision. Advanced practitioners can achieve physical perfection and even take on forms resembling gods.

7. Phytomancy (Plant Magic)

Phytomancy encompasses three distinct subschools of plant magic.

Flora Growth

This subschool accelerates vegetation growth, root manipulation, and vine entrapment.

Practitioners gain command over plant life.

Key abilities include making plants grow rapidly, creating vines that entangle enemies, and shaping trees into forms. Advanced practitioners can create living forests and even world-spanning trees.

Botanical Toxicity

This subschool extracts, synthesizes, and releases plant-based spores, poisons, and hallucinogens. Practitioners learn to weaponize plant toxins.

Key abilities include creating poisonous touches, releasing sleep spores, generating hallucinogenic clouds, and creating deadly toxic blooms. Advanced practitioners can unleash plague blooms that devastate entire regions.

Woodcrafting

This subschool hardens and shapes living timber into armor, weapons, and permanent structures. Practitioners learn to create from living wood.

Key abilities include shaping wood into tools and weapons, creating wood armor and shields, and constructing buildings from living trees. Advanced practitioners can create entire cities from wood.

8. Necromancy (Death Magic)

Necromancy encompasses three distinct subschools of death magic.

Reanimation

This subschool raises and commands mindless undead skeletons, zombies, and corporeal remains. Practitioners learn to call the dead back to service.

Key abilities include creating skeletons and zombies, commanding undead servants, and creating more powerful undead such as ghouls. Advanced practitioners can raise entire undead legions.

Soul Binding

This subschool traps, manipulates, or seals human or creature souls into objects or vessels.

Practitioners learn to handle souls.

Key abilities include trapping souls in gems, containing souls in orbs, exchanging souls between bodies, and harvesting souls from areas. Advanced practitioners can create soul prisons.

Entropic Decay

This subschool accelerates rot, rust, wither, and organic decomposition. Practitioners learn to hasten the end of all things.

Key abilities include causing rust, accelerating decay, withering living matter, and creating zones of entropic destruction. Advanced practitioners can unleash storms of decay.

9. Hemomancy (Blood Magic)

Hemomancy encompasses three distinct subschools of blood magic.

Blood Siphon

This subschool drains vital energy and blood volume from targets to empower the caster or heal wounds. Practitioners learn to feed on the life force of others.

Key abilities include draining blood to heal wounds, siphoning vitality from enemies, and feasting on blood to restore power. Advanced practitioners can drain souls entirely.

Sanguine Crafting

Detailed Accounts

The Great Wars

The First Dark Incursion (3,200 Years Ago)

Duration: 50 years

Region: Azerim, Sol-Kharum

Belligerents: Southern Kingdoms vs. Shadow Forces of the Maw

Cause: Malakor the Betrayer opened the Maw, allowing eldritch horrors and shadow monsters to pour into the mortal plane.

Key Battles

  • Battle of Black Fields: The betrayal that began the war

  • Siege of the White Citadel: The final stand of the Sun-King

  • The Purge of the Maw: The counter-offensive that sealed the rift

Outcome: The shadow forces were pushed back, the Maw was sealed with divine magic, and the Kingdom of Alderkeep was founded to guard against future incursions.

Casualties: Estimated 2 million mortals, countless shadow-creatures.

The Ocean-War of Tides (1,800 Years Ago)

Duration: 30 years

Region: Vael'Tides, Coastal Azerim

Belligerents: Coral Empire vs. Siren High Confederation

Cause: Disputes over deep-sea mining rights and control of trade routes.

Key Battles

  • Battle of the Abyssal Trench: The Coral Empire's naval victory

  • Sinking of the Pearl Fleet: A devastating siren attack

  • The Treaty of Coral-Spire: The peace agreement

Outcome: The Coral Empire established dominance over the deep seas, while the Sirens retained control of surface trade routes.

Casualties: Unknown thousands of merfolk, tritons, and siren casualties; entire sea-cities destroyed.

The Frost-Heart War (400 Years Ago)

Duration: 20 years

Region: Frost-Spire, Northern Azerim

Belligerents: Frost-Lord Czardom, Sub-Glacial Forge Union vs. Wendigo Hordes

Cause: The Wendigo-King's curse spread across the frozen wastes, turning tribes into cannibalistic monsters.

Key Battles

  • Battle of Frozen Tears: The Wendigo-King was sealed

  • Siege of Glacien-Keep: The defense of the capital

  • The Thaw of Ice: The final counter-offensive

Outcome: The Wendigo-King was imprisoned, and the survivors established the Frost-Lord Czardom as the dominant northern power.

Casualties: Over 100,000 frostkin, ice-dwarves, and ursines; the Wendigo hordes were all but annihilated.

The Shattering (900 Years Ago)

Duration: A single catastrophic event

Region: The Shattered Reach (created during this event), Global

Belligerents: None (natural/arcane catastrophe)

Cause: A massive planar rupture caused by a failed celestial summoning in the ancient Sky-Titan Empire.

Effect: The Sky-Titan Empire was shattered into thousands of floating islands. The rupture sent shockwaves across the world, altering weather patterns and opening the first planar rifts in Aethelgard.

Outcome: The Shattered Reach was born as a new continent. The civilization of the Sky-Titans collapsed, leaving behind ruins and anti-gravity crystals that the modern Cloud-Crown Imperium now harvests.

Casualties: The Sky-Titan civilization was wiped out (millions dead). Countless more died from the resulting storms and planar disruptions.

The Obsidian Schism (200 Years Ago)

Duration: 15 years

Region: The Iron Wastes, Global

Belligerents: Obsidian Titanate vs. Iron Wastes Rebellion Forces

Cause: Industrial exploitation of lesser races (goblins, kobolds, gnomes) sparked a massive workers' rebellion.

Key Battles

  • Scrap-Haven Uprising: The rebellion began

  • Siege of Magma-Forge Prime: The turning point

  • The Treaty of the Anvil: The peace agreement

Outcome: The Rust-Guard Syndicate was established as a semi-independent entity within the Iron Wastes. Goblin and kobold workers gained limited rights and autonomy.

Casualties: Estimated 500,000 casualties over fifteen years.

Major Catastrophes

The Plague of Ashes (1,200 Years Ago)

Region: Global (all continents affected)

Cause: A volcanic super-eruption in the Iron Wastes that sent ash across the world.

Effect: Famine, crop failures, and respiratory plagues killed millions. The ash cloud blocked sunlight for three years, creating a dark age that lasted decades.

Survival: The only regions to survive relatively intact were the Deep Underworld, Vael'Tides, and Frost-Spire (which were shielded by geography).

The Trench-Collapse (700 Years Ago)

Region: Vael'Tides

Cause: A massive seismic event that caused the collapse of the Abyssal Trench.

Effect: The collapse released deep-sea pressure waves that destroyed three merfolk cities and shifted ocean currents globally. The event altered global shipping lanes permanently.

Survival: The Coral Empire was devastated but rebuilt over the following centuries.

The Rift-Expansion (120 Years Ago)

Region: Aethelgard

Cause: Unknown planar instability.

Effect: The planar rifts in Aethelgard expanded, allowing void-creatures to leak into the mortal realm. The Celestial Concordat was formed to contain the threat, and the continent was formally settled as a defensive frontier.

Survival: The expansion was contained through the sacrifice of the Radiant Martyr (see Legendary Heroes).

The Moon-Maw Incident (60 Years Ago)

Region: The Dark Spire Dominion

Cause: Attempted sacrificial ritual by Empress Vespera to summon a Void-Lord.

Effect: The ritual was partially successful, tearing open a permanent rift in the sky above the Dark Spire (now called the Moon-Maw). Shadow energy now leaks constantly, empowering Vespera's armies but also creating wild magic zones throughout the northern continent.

Survival: The rift was sealed enough to prevent a full incursion, but the Moon-Maw remains a permanent scar on the sky.
